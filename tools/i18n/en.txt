guide_go|Goal: control more of the board than your opponent. Black moves first.\n• Capture: a group of stones with no empty point next to it (no liberties) is removed from the board.\n• Forbidden: you may not play a move that leaves your own group with no liberties (unless it captures), and you may not repeat an earlier board position.\n• Ending: when your opponent passes and nothing useful is left, tap End game to score the position.\n• Scoring: by area - your stones plus the empty regions only you border. White receives 7.5 komi. Dead stones are not removed automatically, so capture them before you pass.\n• Tap an intersection to preview a stone, then press Place stone.
guide_quoridor|Goal: be the first to reach the opposite side of the board.\n• Move: step one square up, down, left or right. If an opponent's pawn is next to you, jump over it, or step diagonally if a wall or the edge is behind it.\n• Walls: instead of moving you may place a wall two squares long. Walls cannot overlap or cross, and you may never block every path to a goal.\n• Walls left: 10 each with 2 players, 5 each with 4 players.\n• Build: choose Wall, tap near the crossing where it goes to preview it (green = allowed), then press Place wall.
guide_sudoku|Goal: fill every empty square so each row, column and 3×3 box contains the digits 1 to 9 once.\n• Entering: tap a square, then a number. Wrong numbers show in red; fix them with Erase.\n• Notes: switch on the pencil to jot small candidate numbers (shown in red) in a square.\n• Erase clears the selected square. Each 3×3 box starts with only one given number (9 in total), so any grid that follows the rules wins.
guide_wallgo|Goal: end the game with more territory than your rivals.\n• Setup: take turns placing your stones on empty squares (4 each with 2 players, 3 with 3 players, 2 with 4 players).\n• Your turn: choose one of your stones and move it 0, 1 or 2 squares up, down, left or right. It cannot pass through walls or other stones.\n• Wall: after moving you MUST place a wall on one free side of the square you stand on.\n• Territory: when every walled-off area holds stones of only one player, the game ends. You score the squares of the areas that contain only your stones.\n• Ties: the larger single area wins; otherwise it is a draw. A player who cannot move skips the turn.
